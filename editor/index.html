Workflow: load or draw a bank in the editor → pick a bank number → Use editor waves → Upload. The buttons under Module plays switch the module's bank. On the module itself: SPACE menu → EFFECTS (the LED dot shows the bank number).

Editor

Waves 8 × 8. X = SHAPE (+CV), Y = SHAPE in the ORBITS menu (+EFFECTS CV with cv_to_y)

Click to select, Shift+click for a range. Drop a bank .wav onto the grid, or single cycles onto a cell. Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy-paste waves, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo-redo.
