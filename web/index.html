Custom firmware for the Laniakea

Magerit's official firmware, same sound engine, plus the features below. Built in your browser, flashed over USB, calibration kept.

CV → wavetable Y

cv_to_y

Turn any CV jack (e.g. EFFECTS) into a row / Y-axis CV and sweep both wavetable axes by modulation.

Scale quantizer

quantizer

FREQ + V/OCT snap to a scale. Pick scale and root in the SPACE menu: major, minor, pentatonics, dorian, blues and more.

8 wavetable banks

banks

Stored on the module, switched from the SPACE menu. The last bank is restored at power-up.

Custom chord table

chords

CLUSTER chords from the Laniakea Pro set (plus sus2, sus4, add9, min9, maj6), or your own voicings.

Clearer LED menus

led_menus

Each menu gets its own tint, amounts show as bar graphs, and the ring shows the selected bank, bow/pluck and scale changes. With led_dedupe, the ring is only refreshed when it changes.

Usability fixes

usability

Sane factory-reset defaults (no more silence or sub-bass), wider wavetable rows on SHAPE, and 12-semitone FREQ ranges as the manual says.

How it works

  1. Get the official firmware. Open Magerit's repository (mageritsynth/binaries/laniakea), download laniakea_v3.7.bin (or v3.6) and drag it onto step 1. Only v3.6 and v3.7 are supported. Patch addresses are specific to each release.
  2. Pick patches and adjust their values. Recommended is a good start.
  3. Build. Everything runs in this browser, with the same patch files and code as the command-line laniakea patcher. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  4. Flash over USB (Chrome or Edge on desktop, Web Serial), or download the .bin. Your calibration and settings (NVS) are kept.

Power: the module must be powered from the rack. USB alone only powers the USB-serial chip. Connect USB, then switch the rack on. To undo, flash the official image again (step 4 has a button for that). Unofficial, not affiliated with Magerit; flashing modified firmware may void your warranty. Patched images are built for your own module, so please don't redistribute them.
