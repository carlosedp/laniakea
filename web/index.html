What the custom firmware adds

  • Menu crackle fix: the v3.4+ clicking in menus (mostly in additive synth mode) came from the LED ring being redrawn every 8 ms. The ring now only refreshes when something on it changes. Magerit fixed the crackling in firmware 3.7; this patch stays to keep LED updates even lighter.
  • CV → wavetable Y: turn any CV jack (e.g. EFFECTS) into a row/Y-axis CV, so you can sweep both wavetable axes by modulation.
  • Scale quantizer: FREQ + V/OCT snap to a scale. Pick the scale and root in the SPACE menu; 11 scales: major, minor, pentatonics, dorian, blues and more.
  • 8 wavetable banks stored on the module. Switch banks from the SPACE menu; the module remembers the last one at power-up.
  • Custom chord table: the CLUSTER chords can be the Pro's chord set (plus sus2/sus4/add9/min9/maj6) or your own voicings.
  • Clearer LED menus: the SPACE and ORBITS menus each get their own tint, amounts show as bar graphs, and you can see the selected bank, the bow/pluck setting and when the scale changes.
  • Usability fixes: factory-reset defaults no longer make the module sound silent or play sub-bass, the wavetable rows are spread wider on SHAPE, and the FREQ ranges cover 12 semitones as the manual says (stock is 7).
  • Toolkit: full flash backup/restore (keeps your calibration), one-command build and flash, and a framework for writing your own patches. See GitHub.
  • New wavetable editor: browser-based, with AKWF bank building and upload management to the module.

How it works

  1. Get the official firmware. Open Magerit's repository (mageritsynth/binaries/laniakea), download laniakea_v3.7.bin (or v3.6) and drag it onto step 1. Only v3.6 and v3.7 are supported. Patch addresses are specific to each release.
  2. Pick patches and adjust their values. Recommended is a good start.
  3. Build. Everything runs in this browser, with the same patch files and code as the command-line laniakea patcher. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  4. Flash over USB (Chrome or Edge on desktop, Web Serial), or download the .bin. Your calibration and settings (NVS) are kept.

Power: the module must be powered from the rack. USB alone only powers the USB-serial chip. Connect USB, then switch the rack on. To undo, flash the official image again (step 4 has a button for that). Unofficial, not affiliated with Magerit; flashing modified firmware may void your warranty. Patched images are built for your own module, so please don't redistribute them.
